About Naseer Ahmad Bhat

Naseer Ahmad Bhat has been skiing in Gulmarg since he was eight years old — a fact that tells you almost everything about the depth of his relationship with this mountain. Raised here, he developed his skills through years of daily practice long before his professional career began.

What distinguishes Naseer is the breadth of his safety qualifications. He completed international avalanche safety training under certified New Zealand mountain guides and holds his AIARE Level 1 certification to American professional standards. On the slopes, he is relaxed, unhurried and genuinely enjoyable to ski with — equally effective for steady beginners and experienced skiers venturing into backcountry terrain.

Qualifications & Certifications

  • Certified ski instructor — all ability levels
  • AIARE Level 1 Avalanche Certification — American standard
  • International Avalanche Safety Training — New Zealand Guides
  • Official Guide Licence — J&K Himalayan Tourism Department
  • Backcountry terrain experience, full Apharwat range
